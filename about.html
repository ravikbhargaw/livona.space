How We Work

Our 4-Stage Operational Model

01

Enquiry

Tell us your space size, layout, and target product tier online. We evaluate structural feasibility and assign a dedicated project engineer within 2 hours.

02

Site Visit

Our site engineers visit your property in Bangalore with 3D laser scanners to measure exact wall plumb, floor levels, and plumbing line points.

03

Fixed Quote

Receive a comprehensive 3D design render and a line-item bill of quantities within 3-5 days. Every material grade, cost, and delivery date is 100% locked.

04

Execution

Factory production runs off-site while our dedicated project manager manages site prep. Daily photo logs and quality audits ensure seamless handover.
