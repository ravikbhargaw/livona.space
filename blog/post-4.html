Laminate vs Acrylic vs PU: Choosing Your Kitchen Finish

Selecting the external finish for your modular kitchen shutters is a decision that balances aesthetic preference against daily cooking maintenance. Here is a clear comparison across the three most requested finishes.

1. Laminate (1mm High-Pressure Laminate)

Laminates consist of paper sheets pressed with resin under high heat. Available in suede matte, textured woodgrain, or gloss. Highly durable and scratch resistant, making it ideal for rental units or budget-conscious setups.

2. High-Gloss Acrylic

Acrylic sheets are bonded onto HDMR or plywood cores to produce a mirror-like finish. Unlike gloss laminate, high-grade acrylic does not yellow over time under ambient light exposure.

3. Polyurethane (PU) Painted Finish

PU is a liquid paint coating applied in multi-stage spray booths. It eliminates edge-banding tapes completely, producing a continuous, seamless shutter edge.
