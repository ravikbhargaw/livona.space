Laminate vs Acrylic vs PU: Choosing Your Kitchen Finish

Kitchen Finishes Comparison

Selecting cabinet shutter surface finishes is where practical utility meets visual style. Here is a clear comparison of the three dominant modular kitchen finishes used in Indian homes.

1. High-Pressure Laminate (1mm Thick)

Laminates consist of multi-layered paper pressed under heat and pressure. It is extremely tough, heat-resistant, and cost-effective. Ideal for base cabinets exposed to heavy pots.

2. High-Gloss Acrylic Sheets

Acrylic sheets (usually 2mm thick) are vacuum-bonded onto MDF or marine ply. They offer unmatched, mirror-like depth without wave distortions. Best suited for upper wall cabinets.

3. Polyurethane (PU) Lacquer Paint

PU is a liquid paint coating sprayed directly onto routed MDF panels inside dust-free spray booths. Because it coats all edges seamlessly, there are no visible edge-band joints.
