How Much Does a Modular Kitchen Really Cost in Bangalore?

If you have ever asked three different interior designers in Bangalore for a modular kitchen quote, you likely received three wildly conflicting estimates — ranging anywhere from ₹1.2 Lakhs to over ₹4.5 Lakhs for the exact same layout. Why does this happen?

The secret lies in how kitchen costs are calculated. Most traditional interior contractors quote using opaque per-sq.ft rates that hide critical variables like shutter core plywood grade, hardware runner channels, and edge-banding quality until installation day.

1. The Core Material Foundation

In a high-humidity environment like a kitchen, core material selection is non-negotiable. Standard commercial ply (MR grade) will swell when exposed to sink moisture. We mandate Boiling Water Proof (BWP IS 710) marine plywood for all wet modules (sink and dishwasher cabinets), which typically adds 15-20% to raw material cost but ensures a 15-year lifespan.

2. Finish Cost Multipliers

Your choice of external shutter finish determines both aesthetic depth and durability:

  • Laminate (1mm matte/gloss): Most cost-effective entry point (₹1.4L - ₹2.0L range for standard 10x8ft L-shape). Highly scratch-resistant.
  • High-Gloss Acrylic: Seamless glass-like mirror reflectivity (₹2.2L - ₹3.2L range). UV-resistant and anti-yellowing.
  • Polyurethane (PU) Paint: Premium seamless painted shutter with zero visible edge tape (₹3.5L+). Luxurious matte or satin texture.

3. Hardware & Accessories

Surprise cost inflators usually come from internal hardware. Soft-close drawer boxes from premium hardware manufacturers cost significantly more than basic telescopic channels, but guarantee smooth glide performance even under 40kg cookware loads.
