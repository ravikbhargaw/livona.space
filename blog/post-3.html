Interior Fit-Out Timeline: What to Expect Week by Week

Week-by-Week Fit-Out Execution

Managing an interior fit-out can feel overwhelming without a clear roadmap. At Livona Space, we run our projects like software sprints: structured phases, daily status logs, and milestone sign-offs. Here is the exact week-by-week timeline of how a 45-day fit-out unfolds.

Week 1: Site Measurement & Factory Engineering Production

While on-site teams perform laser dimensions, factory CNC machines cut, hot-press laminate sheets, and edge-band cabinet carcasses. Off-site modular joinery cuts site work by 60%.

Week 2: Civil Masonry & Electrical Chipping

On-site electrician and masonry crews chip walls for additional 16A kitchen appliance points, TV unit cabling conduits, and false ceiling electrical wiring feeds.

Week 3: False Ceiling Framing & Flat-Pack Unloading

Gyproc metal channels are installed and plasterboard sheets fixed. Carcass flat-packs arrive on site with protective plastic wrapping for inventory sign-off.

Week 4-5: Modular Assembly & Countertop Template

Cabinets, wardrobes, and kitchen carcasses are assembled level-aligned. Granite or Quartz fabricators measure exact templates for kitchen sink cutting.

Week 6: Painting, Deep Cleaning & Handover

Final acrylic putty sanding, top-coat emulsion, LED fixture testing, and deep chemical cleaning prior to key handover.
