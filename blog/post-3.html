Interior Fit-Out Timeline: What to Expect Week by Week

One of the biggest friction points in home renovation is timeline uncertainty. At Livona Space, we run our fit-out operations on a rigid 45-day execution pipeline so homeowners can plan move-in dates with absolute confidence.

Week 1–2: Laser Measurement & Factory CNC Production

While site engineers execute electrical conduit wall chasing and ceiling marking on site, our automated off-site CNC joinery plants cut, edge-band, and pre-drill all cabinet carcasses to sub-millimeter precision.

Week 3–4: Carcass Assembly & Countertop Fitting

Flat-pack cabinetry arrives on site in protective wrapping. Carcasses are mounted, leveled, and fixed. Quartz countertops are cut and installed with seamless undermount sinks.

Week 5: False Ceiling, Electrical & Primer Paint

Moisture-resistant ceiling channels are framed and wired for spot COB lights and cove LED strips. Wall putty and acrylic primer coats are applied.

Week 6: Shutter Fitting, Deep Clean & Handover Audit

External shutters (laminate, acrylic, or PU) are aligned with soft-close hinges. Final coat of washable paint is applied followed by professional deep cleaning and quality checklist sign-off.
