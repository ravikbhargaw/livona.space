Planning a Bathroom Renovation? Start With These 3 Questions

Bathroom Planning Fundamentals

Renovating a bathroom is the most technically concentrated project in any home. Before falling in love with a ceramic tile pattern on Pinterest, ask your project engineer these three core questions.

1. Is the Existing Floor Slab Sunken or Raised?

In older apartments in Bangalore, plumbing lines run through sunken slabs. In newer high-rises, pipes run through the floor below inside a false ceiling. Knowing your slab type dictates whether wall-hung WCs and concealed flush tanks can be recessed easily.

2. Are CPVC/PEX Plumbing Pipes Older Than 10 Years?

Never lay new ₹250/sq.ft tiles over 15-year-old GI or degraded PVC pipes. Upgrading to heavy-gauge CPVC or multi-layer composite pipes before tiling guarantees you won't need to break tiles open 3 years later.

3. What is the Dedicated Dry vs Wet Zone Layout?

Creating a distinct wet zone (shower enclosure) and dry zone (vanity & WC) reduces daily cleaning by 80% and extends cabinet laminate life indefinitely.
