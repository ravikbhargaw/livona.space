Planning a Bathroom Renovation? Start With These 3 Questions

Before falling in love with a specific tile pattern or black matte fitting, evaluate these critical structural constraints during your initial site measurement.

1. What is the Condition & Age of Existing Concealed Pipes?

If your residence is over 10 years old, existing GI or low-grade PVC plumbing should be replaced with high-pressure CPVC/PPR piping before laying new wall tiles.

2. Can the Floor Slab Accommodate a Wall-Hung EWC Cistern?

Installing a wall-hung WC requires either creating a granite-topped ledge wall or chasing into existing brickwork to fit a concealed flush cistern.

3. How Will Wet and Dry Zones Be Separated?

A simple 8mm toughened glass partition prevents shower water from splashing onto vanity cabinets, doubling the lifespan of wooden furniture.
