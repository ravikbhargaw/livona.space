False Ceiling Types Explained: POP vs Gypsum vs PVC

False Ceiling Comparison

False ceilings are more than decorative elements — they hide AC ducting, electrical wiring conduits, and ambient LED light drivers while providing acoustic insulation.

1. Plaster of Paris (POP)

POP is mixed on-site and applied over chicken wire mesh. While highly customizable for intricate curved moldings, it is labor-intensive, slow to dry, and creates substantial site dust.

2. Pre-Fabricated Gypsum Board Panels

Gypsum board panels (like Saint-Gobain Gyproc) are screwed onto galvanized steel framing grid lines. They install 3x faster than POP, produce zero messy slurry, and offer flawless flat seam joints.

3. PVC & Grid Tiles for Wet Zones

In bathrooms or balcony overhangs, PVC tongue-and-groove planks or moisture-resistant MR gypsum boards prevent humidity sagging and mold growth.
