False Ceiling Types Explained: POP vs Gypsum vs PVC

A false ceiling conceals electrical wires, supports architectural recessed spotlighting, and adds acoustic insulation. Here is how the three main materials compare.

1. Gypsum Board Sheets

Pre-fabricated gypsum boards screw directly onto GI metal channels. They allow clean, fast installation with minimal dust and superior joint cracking resistance.

2. Plaster of Paris (POP)

Applied as a wet paste over wire mesh. Highly customizable for complex curved moldings, but requires skilled manual application and extended drying time.

3. PVC Panels

Water-resistant synthetic panels suitable for high-moisture utility balconies or damp bathroom ceilings.
