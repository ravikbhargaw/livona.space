Standard Pipeline

Our Standardized 5-Step Process

Used for every customer across all project scales for predictable execution.

01

Virtual Consultation

Share your requirements and floor plan with our team over a call or video consultation.

02

Mood Board & Preliminary Quote

We build a mood board and give you a preliminary quote based on your floor plan.

03

Design Token

A token payment moves your project into detailed design, handled by our design partner team.

04

Design Phase

Detailed design finalized with your feedback and approval.

05

Execution

Our team visits your site to kick off and manage execution end to end.

💡
Process & Site Visit Note:

For standardized packages (Bathroom Renovation, Kitchen Package, Bedroom Package, Living Room Package), your quote is finalized remotely based on your floor plan — no site visit needed until execution. For fully customized or bespoke projects, we offer a free in-person site visit before you commit to the design token, to ensure accurate scope and pricing.
