Frequently Asked Questions

Bathroom Renovation FAQ

Our 3-layer elastomeric membrane requires 24 hours between coats and a mandatory 48-hour standing water pond test before tile adhesives are applied.
While tile-over-tile is technically possible, we advise a complete strip-down to inspect underlying plumbing and guarantee 100% leak protection.
We isolate work areas using heavy-duty floor-to-ceiling zip curtains and negative-air dust extractors to keep living spaces completely clean.
Every Livona Space bathroom renovation comes with an official 5-year leak-free warranty certificate.
